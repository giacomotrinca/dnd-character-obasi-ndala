\subsection*{Ring of Spell Storing} (Rare, attunement)
This ring stores spells cast into it, holding them until the attuned
wearer uses them. The ring can store up to 5 levels worth of spells at a
time. When found, it contains 1d6$-1$ levels of stored spells chosen by
the DM. Any creature can cast a spell of 1st-5th level into the ring by
touching the ring as the spell is cast. The spell has no effect, other
than to be stored in the ring. If the ring can't hold the spell, the
spell is expended without effect. The level of the slot used to cast the
spell determines how much space it uses. While wearing this ring, you can
cast any spell stored in it. The spell uses the slot level, spell save
DC, spell attack bonus, and spellcasting ability of the original caster,
but is otherwise treated as if you cast the spell. The spell cast from
the ring is no longer stored in it, freeing up space.

\subsection*{Pearl of Power} (Uncommon, attunement)
While this pearl is on your person, you can use an action to speak its
command word and regain one expended spell slot. If the expended slot is
of 4th level or higher, the new slot is 3rd level. Once you have used the
pearl, it can't be used again until the next dawn.

\subsection*{Cloak of Protection} (Uncommon, attunement)
You gain a $+1$ bonus to AC and to saving throws while you wear this
cloak.

\subsection*{Portable Hole} (Rare)
This fine black cloth, soft as silk, is folded up to the dimensions of a
handkerchief. It unfolds into a circular sheet 1.8 meters in diameter.
You can use an action to unfold it and place it over or against a solid
surface, whereupon the portable hole creates an extradimensional hole
3 meters deep. The cylindrical space within the hole exists on a
different plane, so it has no effect on the space it's in and can't open
passages. Any creature or object within its reach can fit inside. As long
as the hole remains open, you can use it as a hideout. You can use an
action to close it by folding the cloth closed, and any creatures or
objects within remain inside. If closed with a creature inside, it can
use an action to make a Strength check DC 10 to force its way out and
emerge within 1.5 meters of the cloth or its carrier. A breathing
creature inside a closed portable hole can survive up to 10 minutes, then
begins to suffocate. Placing the portable hole inside an extradimensional
space (such as that of a bag of holding) instantly destroys both items
and opens a gate to the Astral Plane.

Lightning springs from your hand to deliver a shock to a creature you try to touch. Make a melee spell attack against the target. The spell's damage increases by 1d8 when you reach 5th level (2d8), 11th level (3d8), and 17th level (4d8).

This spell's damage increases by 1d10 when you reach 5th level (2d10), 11th level (3d10), and 17th level (4d10).

\DndSpellHeader{Chill Touch}{Necromancy cantrip}{1 action}{120 feet}{V, S}{1 round}

On a hit, the target takes 1d8 necrotic damage, and it can't regain hit points until the start of your next turn. Until then, the hand clings to the target.

If you hit an undead target, it also has disadvantage on attack rolls against you until the end of your next turn.

This spell's damage increases by 1d8 when you reach 5th level (2d8), 11th level (3d8), and 17th level (4d8).

An affected target is so convinced of the phantasm's reality that it can even take damage from the illusion. A phantasm created to appear as a creature can attack the target. Each round on your turn, the phantasm can deal 1d6 psychic damage to the target if it is in the phantasm's area or within 5 feet of the phantasm, provided that the illusion is of a creature or hazard that could logically deal damage, such as by attacking. The target perceives the damage as a type appropriate to the illusion.

\chapter*{The Character}

\DndDropCapLine{O}{basi N'Dala} is a wizard of the Order of Scribes,
originally raised as an \emph{Acolyte}; his mind is a blade as sharp as
his pen.

\section{Biography}
Born in the slums, Obasi always knew that magic is not a mystical gift
but an exact science, a weapon that demands discipline and ruthlessness.
In secret he is \textbf{a master of the cult of Mystra}, the Mother of
All Magic: for him the Weave is not merely a tool but the sacred fabric
of the universe. His path began when he crossed the trail of the
\emph{Master Scribe}, an old and cynical mage who worked in the shadows,
secretly affiliated with the Black Network (Zhentarim). It was he who
taught Obasi that the Weave is a perfect tapestry, and that anyone who
reaches into it without study --- like those abominations, the sorcerers,
who vomit magic by pure genetic chance --- is a dangerous anomaly to be
studied, caged, and neutralized. Only wizards deserve power.

Under the Master's guidance, Obasi became a member of the Order of
Scribes and learned to channel his magical essence into a seemingly
harmless object. With his relaxed demeanor, long gray-streaked dreadlocks,
and always low, calm voice, Obasi chose a simple bone hairpin that kept
his locks gathered. One fluid gesture of his hand slides the ornament
from his hair and, blazing with a spectral energy, turns it into his
Wizard's Pen --- an instrument of creation and of death.

His composure and ruthlessness drew the attention of the Black Network's
leadership, who infiltrated him among the ranks of Waterdeep's magical
mercenaries. Obasi played his part flawlessly: a taciturn mage, useful
but seemingly bereft of political ambition. A profile so perfect that it
caught the eye of Vajra Safahr, the current Blackstaff.

Vajra, however, is no fool. She saw in Obasi an all-too-capable and
potentially dangerous individual, the classic ``undesirable subject''.
Not suspecting his Zhentarim affiliation in the slightest, she decided
to use him as cannon fodder: she summoned him in secret and gave him a
suicide mission --- infiltrate Xanathar's lair and wipe out the beholder
that controlled Waterdeep's back alleys. Obasi accepted with a nod, not
to obey the Blackstaff but because he knew that among the beholder's
trophies lay the \textbf{phylactery of a lich}, hidden in a ring owned by
Xanathar, which the Black Network and his masters in the cult of Mystra
wanted recovered at any cost.

Against all the Blackstaff's expectations, Obasi succeeded: the guild
was shattered and the phylactery returned to the right hands. It was
spring of 1492 DR, shortly after the \emph{Dragon Heist}, and the streets
of Waterdeep already trembled with the war between Xanathar's guild and
the Black Network. When Obasi returned, with his usual composure and an
unfaltering voice, Vajra realized she had an even bigger problem on her
hands. With a feigned smile of satisfaction, the Blackstaff offered him a
``promotion'': the definitive mission for a hero --- descend into
Undermountain, find the mad Halaster Blackcloak, and free Waterdeep from
his threat forever.

For this task Obasi gathered a party: \textbf{Yelena}, a rogue who
casually blends daggers and spells --- he saw her work magic and was
fascinated, and a good rapport has grown between them; she is also
training him in the use of the blade; \textbf{Varis}, an exceptional
archer who has saved his life more than once and enjoys his full trust;
\textbf{Opa}, a dwarven life cleric who is teaching him the ways of the
shield; and \textbf{Theren}, a human about whom he knows little and, by
instinct, does not fully trust.

Vajra is convinced she has sent him to die in the greatest meat-grinder
of Faerûn. She does not know that Obasi smiled at the offer. The mission
fits perfectly with his plan and the interests of the Black Network: he
will descend into Undermountain, yes. He will kill Halaster, certainly.
But not to free Waterdeep. Because Undermountain, soaked with magic to
its very marrow, is draining the Weave across this whole part of Faerûn
--- and if the draining continues, the Weave itself will tear and
\textbf{Mystra will die}. Obasi will descend to kill the Mad Mage and
restore the fabric of the Weave, then claim Undermountain for himself,
seizing the vast magical knot hidden within, before rising to the surface,
crushing the Blackstaff, toppling the institutions that restrain wizards,
and founding a new order where magic is controlled only by those with the
right to do so.

\section{Personality traits}
I always speak in a low, calm, and relaxed tone of voice, even while
unleashing lethal magic. My peaceful demeanor and unkempt appearance
hide a calculating and ruthless mind.

\section{Ideals}
Magic is the supreme force of the universe and must be mastered only by
those with the discipline to study it. Those born with innate powers are
an anomaly of nature and must be caged or eliminated.

\section{Bonds}
My quill-hairpin is my most trusted companion, the extension of my mind.
The Black Network is the tool through which I will bring order to
Waterdeep and take Undermountain.

\section{Flaws}
My intellectual pride is immense: I systematically underestimate
sorcerers and innate spellcasters, treating them as undisciplined animals.
Moreover, my ambition to rule Undermountain blinds me to the true,
unfathomable madness of that place.
